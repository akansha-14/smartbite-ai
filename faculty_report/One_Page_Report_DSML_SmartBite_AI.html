TITLE OF PROJECT – SMARTBITE AI: SMART CAMPUS FOOD WASTE PREDICTION AND OPTIMIZATION SYSTEM
SDG Mapped – SDG 12: Responsible Consumption & Production (Target 12.3), SDG 2: Zero Hunger, SDG 13: Climate Action
Abstract :

Institutional cafeterias face significant operational challenges in balancing bulk food production against volatile diner attendance, resulting in avoidable food surplus, economic loss, and landfill methane emissions. SmartBite AI delivers an end-to-end supervised machine learning system engineered to predict pre-service food waste (in kilograms) before cooking shifts commence. Utilizing a synthetic operational dataset of 5,000 cafeteria records strictly grounded in physical mass conservation (Food Prepared = Food Consumed + Food Wasted) with pre-meal feature quarantine, four regression algorithms—Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting—were benchmarked on an 80/20 train-test split. The Gradient Boosting Regressor achieved champion predictive accuracy with a Test Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of 15.08 kg, Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) of 19.34 kg, and an R² score of 0.806, explaining 80.6% of waste variance and significantly outperforming the baseline linear model (R² = 0.655). Feature importance analysis indicates food preparation volume, meal slot, historical attendance, and menu category as primary waste drivers. Deployed through an interactive Streamlit graphical interface with staged cooking schedules, the system equips dining managers with proactive decision support to curb batch surplus, advancing UN Sustainable Development Goals 12, 2, and 13.

Objectives:
  1. To formulate a realistic cafeteria operational dataset adhering to mass conservation and strict anti-leakage pre-meal quarantine.
  2. To engineer operational predictors and benchmark four supervised ML algorithms (Linear Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting).
  3. To evaluate model generalization using standard metrics (MAE, RMSE, R²) and extract feature importance governing food surplus.
  4. To deploy an interactive Streamlit graphical interface enabling real-time waste predictions, staged batch cooking, and SDG impact quantification.
